# 170V Nixie Tube Boost Converter

This is a custom PCB designed from scratch in KiCad 8. It takes a 5V input and boosts it up to 170V DC to power Nixie tubes. 

## The Design
* **Controller:** MC34063 
* **Switch:** External N-Channel MOSFET (for high voltage handling)
* **Layout:** 2-Layer PCB with a full bottom-layer Ground Plane for noise reduction.
* **High Voltage Routing:** Thick traces with high clearance for the 170V output.
